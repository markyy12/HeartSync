FAQ

What if I'm not sure about the desired qualities of my future partner?
Our skilled matchmakers and psychologists work directly with you to uncover core preferences and values to build a comprehensive relationship profile.
Where do dates usually go? What about the safety issues?
All initial dates take place in curated, public, upscale venues. Background validation checks are run to ensure complete comfort and absolute safety.
What if I don't like the chosen candidate after a few dates?
That is completely fine! Feedback is collected after every intro to refine choices, and new candidates are continually sourced.
How do I choose the perfect match? When should I stop?
You stop whenever you feel genuine connection and exclusivity with someone. We're here to guide but you set the pace.
Can I take expensive presents or agree to go on vacations together?
We suggest maintaining balanced, independent boundaries during early stages until solid reciprocal understanding is established.
When do I pay for your services? What are the options for matchmaking?
Pricing tiers are detailed cleanly during consultation. Plans depend on customization parameters and regional search ranges.
